# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Scope: the participant experience of the Stewardship programme — a responsive web
application in which participants learn to build redemptive technology through a
structured curriculum, learning modules, and 1-on-1 sessions with a mentor.

The mentor and administrator experience — authoring curriculum, managing cohorts,
setting mentor availability, reviewing participant progress — is out of scope for this
revision and will be added as a later set of L1s.

---

## L1-001: Account and access
The system must establish who a participant is before granting access to any programme
content, must keep that identity across visits without requiring repeated proof, and
must let a participant end their access deliberately from whatever device they are
using. No programme content is reachable by an unidentified visitor.

## L1-002: Cohort enrollment
The system must place each participant in exactly one cohort. The cohort determines
which curriculum the participant follows, the dates over which the programme runs, the
cadence of their 1-on-1 sessions, and which mentor they meet. A participant who belongs
to no cohort must be told so rather than shown an empty programme.

## L1-003: Curriculum progression
The system must present the curriculum as an ordered path of modules, show a
participant their standing on that path at a glance, and govern which modules are open
to them. Every count and proportion the system displays about progress must be derived
from recorded completion, never authored separately.

## L1-004: Module delivery and completion
The system must deliver a module as an ordered set of sections carrying reading
material and a practice assignment, must let a participant record a section as
complete, and must present the same assignment content to a given participant
regardless of the device they read it on.

## L1-005: One-on-one session scheduling
The system must show a participant the times their mentor has open, let them book one,
let them change or cancel that booking within a stated window, and show them the
sessions they have already held. The number of sessions a cohort includes must follow
from the cohort's duration and session cadence.

## L1-006: Notes and session preparation
The system must let a participant write, revise, and retrieve notes attached to a
module or to a session, and must carry a module's preparation prompts through to the
session they are meant to inform. Notes are private to the participant and their
mentor.

## L1-007: Responsive presentation
The system must present every participant-facing screen usably from extra-small
(<576px) through extra-large (>=1200px) viewports. Every content element, action, and
destination available at one viewport size must remain reachable at every other.

## L1-008: Accessibility
The system must be operable by participants who navigate by keyboard, rely on assistive
technology, or read with reduced vision. Meaning the interface conveys through colour,
size, or position alone must also be available in text or structure.

## L1-009: Security
The system must protect participant identity, notes, and progress against unauthorised
access, disclosure, and tampering, must validate everything it accepts from a client,
and must defend against the OWASP Top Ten classes of attack.

## L1-010: Performance and operations
The system must respond quickly enough that reading a module and booking a session feel
uninterrupted, and must emit the health, error, and audit signals needed to operate and
diagnose it in production.
